# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: being kind and respectful, giving and gracefully
accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for our mistakes, focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without their
explicit permission, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainers
through a [GitHub security advisory / private report](https://github.com/Hardin22/SmartChessboard/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the repository owner on GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly,
and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
The full text is available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
